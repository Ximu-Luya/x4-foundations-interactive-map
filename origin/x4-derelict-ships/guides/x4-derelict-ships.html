Guide 01 · Free Ships

X4 Abandoned & Derelict Ships
and Locations

X4: Foundations is full of abandoned ships, ownerless derelicts just sitting there, from cheap scouts to full destroyers. Fly out, claim them in your spacesuit, and they're yours for free. Below: how claiming works, the best free ships ranked, every known location, and the nine Timelines reward ships (TL1 to TL9).

See every free ship on the interactive X4 universe map
The Shortlist

The best free, derelict & abandoned ships in X4: Foundations

All six are free, but they aren't equal. Here are the abandoned & derelict ships ranked by what you get for the trip, from a full destroyer down to early-game scouts. Tap any name for exact coordinates.

  1. 1
    Odysseus Vanguard L · Destroyer ★ Best free ship

    Faulty Logic VII. A full L-class destroyer that spawns fully equipped, since 4.0 HF2 you claim it solo by spacewalking to the bridge. The single best free ship in the game.

  2. 2
    Osprey Vanguard M · Frigate Easiest medium

    Company Regard. A free medium frigate you claim straight from your spacesuit. The best low-effort upgrade on the list.

  3. 3
    Drill Vanguard M · Miner

    The Void. A free M-class miner, a handy free mining ship. Spacesuit claim.

  4. 4
    Elite Vanguard S · Interceptor

    Grand Exchange I. A fast early-game interceptor, quick spacesuit claim.

  5. 5
    Perseus Vanguard S · Fighter

    Silent Witness XII. A free S fighter on the ecliptic, an easy spacesuit claim.

  6. 6
    Courier Vanguard S · Courier

    Nopileos' Fortune II. A free small courier near the highway exit, a handy starter hull.

The Basics

How to claim a derelict

// Every ship size, S to XL
  1. 01 · Fly to the ship and stop close to it.
  2. 02 · Leave your ship, get out into your spacesuit (EVA).
  3. 03 · Press Shift+2, find the red pulsing light on the hull and repair it with your spacesuit. After the repair the ship is claimed automatically.
  4. 04 · Assign a pilot or fly it home yourself.

How the claim works: A derelict is ownerless, so you simply take it: fly out, EVA in your spacesuit, and repair the red pulsing light. After the repair it is yours. Same method for every ship size, from S to XL.

Reset note: Most of these are one-time per save: once you claim one it is gone from that spot. The locations are fixed, they do not move between patches.
