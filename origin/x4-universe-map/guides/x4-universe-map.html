        About this map

        The interactive X4 Foundations galaxy map, every sector, all DLCs

        Every sector in the current X4: Foundations galaxy with all expansions installed, Split Vendetta, Cradle of Humanity, Tides of Avarice and Kingdom End. Hexes are coloured by their controlling faction; lines are gate links, dashed lines are super-highways. Click any sector for its faction and connections.

        Use it as a complete X4 sector map and galaxy map: search any system by name, then pan and zoom from the whole galaxy down to a single sector, every base-game and DLC region on one interactive map.

        Flip on the Derelict Ships lens (top toolbar) to mark every sector with a claimable derelict, pin colour shows the ship's class (S/M/L), and each marker opens its claim notes, coordinates and a link straight to the full derelict ships guide. Click any sector and hit Plan a route here, or use Plan a Route in the top toolbar to pick a start and destination by name, for a gate-by-gate path between any two systems.         FAQ

        X4 map & free ships, quick answers

        Where do I find free ships in X4: Foundations?

        Free, derelict and abandoned ships are ownerless wrecks across the galaxy. Confirmed spots: Grand Exchange I, Silent Witness XII, Nopileos' Fortune II, Company Regard, The Void and Faulty Logic VII. Toggle the Free Ships lens on the map above to see every sector.

        What is the best free ship in X4?

        The Odysseus Vanguard, an L-class destroyer in Faulty Logic VII. Since 4.0 HF2 it spawns fully equipped, so you can claim it by spacewalking to the bridge.

        How do I claim a derelict ship in X4?

        Leave your ship in a spacesuit, fly to the wreck and repair the red pulsing light on the hull. Once repaired the ship is yours. This works the same for every ship size.

        Does this X4 map include all DLCs?

        Yes, the full universe with Split Vendetta, Cradle of Humanity, Tides of Avarice and Kingdom End installed, current for the v9.0 sector layout. Resource data (ore, silicon, ice, gases, scrap) follows after the X4 9.0 update.
